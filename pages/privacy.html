Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

At LuminaLab, we take your privacy and the security of your health information very seriously. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us, including your name, contact details, date of birth, and health-related information necessary for performing diagnostic tests and providing medical reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for:

  • Processing your diagnostic tests.
  • Delivering your test results through our secure Patient Portal.
  • Communicating with you regarding your appointments.

3. Data Security (HIPAA Compliance)

We implement advanced security measures designed to protect your personal and health information. All data transmitted through our portal is encrypted, and we strictly adhere to HIPAA regulations.
