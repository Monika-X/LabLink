How it works

  1. Upload a clear photo or scan of your valid doctor's prescription.
  2. Our medical team will review the prescription within 1 hour.
  3. We will create a custom cart with the exact tests prescribed.
  4. You will receive an email to review the cart, select a time slot, and make payment.
